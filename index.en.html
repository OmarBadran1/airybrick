About Our Factory

Our Story

Founded in 1985 by the Petrov family, Volgograd AirBrick began as a small operation with a big vision: to create the highest quality airy bricks in Southern Russia. What started with traditional kilns and hand-crafted processes has evolved into a modern facility that still maintains the artisanal quality our customers expect.

Our airy bricks are known for their exceptional thermal insulation properties, lightweight construction, and durability that stands the test of time. Each brick is carefully crafted using locally sourced clay and our proprietary aeration process.
